About The Den

More Than A Gym.

Bobby's Iron Den is built around one simple idea: consistent training produces stronger people.

Whether you're beginning your fitness journey or already experienced in the gym, the Den provides an environment where training, discipline and progression come first.

Located in Ogbomoso, Nigeria, Bobby's Iron Den is designed to be a focused suburban fitness space for people who are serious about improving themselves.
