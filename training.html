Train
Hard.
Train Smart.

A structured Monday–Friday training split designed around strength, hypertrophy, progressive overload and focused muscle development.

Start Monday View Split
Weekly Structure

Five Days. One System.

Each training day has a specific purpose. Follow the sequence, train with control and prioritize progressive improvement.

Monday
Back + Triceps
Heavy pulling
Tuesday
Chest + Shoulders
Pressing + delts
Wednesday
Legs + Calves
Lower-body strength
Thursday
Back + Biceps
Width + thickness
Friday
Shoulders + Arms
Hypertrophy
Day 01 • Monday

Back + Triceps 🔥

Main Goal: Heavy pulling

Training Focus
Strength + Pulling Power
Weighted Pull-ups
Back • Biceps
4 × 6–8
Barbell Rows
4 × 6–8
Lat Pulldown
3 × 8–12
Straight-Arm Pulldown
3 × 10–15
Close-Grip Bench Press
3 × 6–10
Overhead Cable Triceps Extension
3 × 10–15
Rope Pushdown
2–3 × 12–15

Chest + Shoulders 💪

Main Goal: Pressing + delts

Barbell Bench Press
4 × 5–8
Incline Dumbbell Press
3 × 8–10

3 × 10–12
Overhead Press
3 × 6–10
Dumbbell Lateral Raises
4 × 12–20
Face Pulls
3 × 12–20

Legs + Calves 🦵

Back + Biceps 🔥

Day 05 • Friday

Shoulders + Arms 💥

Main Goal: Hypertrophy/finishing work
